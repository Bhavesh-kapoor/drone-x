Our biggest differentiator — our management team

Expertise that goes beyond conventional service delivery.

At DroneX, we believe that technology delivers its greatest value when guided by the right expertise. Our management team brings together professionals from complementary disciplines, creating a strong foundation for delivering integrated and specialised services.

01

Facilities & Property Management Experts

Our core management strength comes from experienced professionals with extensive exposure to Facilities Management, Property Management, commercial real estate operations and infrastructure management.

Their collective industry understanding encompasses building operations, technical maintenance, asset management, service delivery, vendor governance, statutory compliance, transition management, operational risk management and cost optimisation.

This experience enables us to understand client requirements beyond the scope of individual services and develop solutions aligned with the overall operational and commercial objectives of an asset.

Qualified Fire & Safety Professionals
