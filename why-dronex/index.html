Technology alone cannot deliver better building outcomes.

Building expertise. Drone innovation. Operational excellence.

DroneX Facility Services brings together the precision of drone technology and the practical knowledge of experienced property and facility management professionals.

Our approach is grounded in building services, façade systems, cleaning methodology, water management, safety and compliance, tenant relationships and the operational realities of commercial, residential and mixed-use developments.

“What does the building actually require, and how can the job be delivered safely, efficiently and without disrupting its operations?”

The question DroneX starts every assignment with

What makes DroneX different?

Conventional drone services typically start with the drone — its payload, flight operations and technical capability. We start with the building.

01

Building-first, not drone-first

We begin with the building’s façade materials, access constraints, operating environment, water supply, drainage and maintenance requirements — and only then select the appropriate drone technology and operating method.

02

Deep property and facility management expertise

Our leadership understands the practical realities of building operations: MEP services, maintenance planning, service contracts, vendor coordination, asset protection and operational continuity.

03

Cleaning science meets drone capability

Effective façade cleaning requires more than a flying platform. Cleaning agents, dilution, surface compatibility, water quality, flow rates, operating pressure, nozzle selection and rinsing methods are evaluated for the specific building and cleaning objective.

04

Risk, safety and compliance built into execution

Each assignment is approached through site-specific risk assessment, method statements, exclusion zones, weather evaluation, emergency procedures and the applicable aviation, building, environmental and occupational safety requirements.

05

Seamless coordination with building stakeholders

We coordinate with property managers, engineering teams, security, housekeeping, occupants, tenants and other service providers to minimise disruption and protect the building’s daily operations.

06

Performance verification and asset protection

Success is not measured by flight performance alone. Each assignment has a defined scope, quality checks, documented observations and service reporting — with recommendations where corrective action is needed.

Our integrated building expertise

Six disciplines that sit behind every DroneX assignment.

  • Façade and building intelligence

    Façade materials, access constraints, condition surveys, sensitive surfaces and building-specific cleaning plans.

  • Cleaning and water engineering

    Cleaning chemistry, water supply, pumping arrangements, flow rates, operating pressure and wastewater management.

  • Safety and compliance

    Site-specific risk assessments, operating procedures, safety controls, emergency planning and applicable regulatory requirements.

  • Property and tenant coordination

    Work permits, security interfaces, tenant communication, access planning and minimising operational disruption.

  • Drone surveillance and inspection

    Planned visual surveys, façade observations, roof inspections and other monitoring suited to the equipment and permissions available.

  • Performance and asset protection

    Defined scopes, quality checks, documented observations, service reporting and continuous improvement.
