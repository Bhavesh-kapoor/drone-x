Why DroneX

Technology alone cannot deliver better building outcomes.

Operating a drone is one skill. Planning a façade service that protects the asset, respects the occupants and fits the building’s operations is another.

DroneX brings both together — so the equipment serves the building, not the other way round.

Discover Why DroneX

  • Building-first assessment

    Façade materials, access, water supply, drainage and maintenance needs come before equipment choice.

  • Property and facility management expertise

    Practical knowledge of MEP services, maintenance planning, vendors and operational continuity.

  • Cleaning method and surface compatibility

    Cleaning agents, dilution, water quality, pressure and rinsing chosen for the specific surface.

  • Site-specific risk assessment

    Method statements, exclusion zones, weather and emergency arrangements planned for each site.

  • Stakeholder and occupant coordination

  • Quality verification and reporting

    Completion is documented, quality reviewed and observations recorded for the client.

How we work

The DroneX five-step delivery framework applies to every assignment, from routine facility services to façade work at height.

  1. 1

    Understand the building

    Façade type, site conditions, operational constraints and client expectations.

  2. 2

    Engineer the solution

    The right equipment, cleaning system, water delivery and working method.

  3. 3

    Assess risk and authorise work

    Risk assessment, method statement, permits, exclusion zones and applicable approvals.

  4. 4

    Execute with coordination

    Delivery alongside property management, engineering, security and tenant teams.

  5. 5

    Verify and report

    Documented completion, quality review, observations and corrective recommendations.

Industries we serve

Safety, quality and operational coordination

Work on an occupied building is only as good as its planning. Every DroneX assignment is prepared for the specific site before work begins.

  • Site-specific planning. Access, work zones, timing and building systems reviewed on site.
  • Appropriate authorisations. Permits and approvals identified and obtained as the site and activity require.
  • Risk assessment. Hazards to people, property and the public assessed, with controls set out in a method statement.
  • Work coordination. Agreed with the property team, security and occupants to limit disruption.
  • Quality checks. Results reviewed against the agreed scope and documented for the client.
