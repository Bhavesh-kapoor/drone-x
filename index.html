Safety first

Safety, quality and operational coordination

Work on an occupied building is only as good as its planning. Every DroneX assignment is prepared for the specific site before work begins.

Our safety and quality approach

  • Site-specific planning. Access, work zones, timing and building systems reviewed on site.
  • Appropriate authorisations. Permits and approvals identified and obtained as the site and activity require.
  • Risk assessment. Hazards to people, property and the public assessed, with controls set out in a method statement.
  • Work coordination. Agreed with the property team, security and occupants to limit disruption.
  • Quality checks. Results reviewed against the agreed scope and documented for the client.

Drone-as-a-Service (DaaS) – Franchise Model Across Multiple Use Cases

Dronex aims to develop a scalable Drone-as-a-Service (DaaS) franchise ecosystem, enabling entrepreneurs, service partners and business associates to leverage established drone technologies across multiple commercial, industrial and infrastructure applications.

Through collaboration with established Indian drone manufacturers and technology partners, we intend to create a structured franchise model that provides access to specialised drone platforms, application-specific equipment, operational methodologies, technical support and business development opportunities.

The model will extend beyond façade cleaning to encompass multiple drone-enabled service applications, including infrastructure inspection, solar panel maintenance, industrial cleaning, building maintenance and other emerging commercial use cases.

  • Façade cleaning
  • Infrastructure inspection
  • Solar panel maintenance
  • Industrial cleaning
  • Building maintenance

Our objective is to create a scalable service network that combines indigenous drone technology, standardised operating practices, professional training and local execution capabilities.

This positions Dronex not merely as a drone service provider, but as a potential technology-enabled franchise and service delivery platform.
